About Nagomi

Built around being yourself.

We are a relaxed community where all are safe to be as they wish. We believe in awareness and education on mental health and lgbtqia+ matters. We love and value the power of having a space where you feel comfortable, which is why our goal is to not only educate but to also create a space where you can find a sense of belonging and deep community.

Server

Our community

  • Awareness
    We have channels with resources and post awareness content throughout the server.
  • Variety
    We offer a variety of topics for you to chose from. With seperate channels and discussions.
  • Belonging
    We have an active, caring community where everyone is welcome. With friendly members and kind staff we are always doing our best to create the best environment we can for everyone.
