Some common questions and queries members have.

What is Nagomi?

Nagomi is a Discord community focused on mental health, Pride, anime and gaming.

How do I apply for staff?

You can apply for staff from directly within the server. If you are interested please check under desk for the apply channel. From there you can start your application.

Can I be a developer for Nagomi?

It is rare for us to take on any devs but if you are really keen you can dm our owner.
