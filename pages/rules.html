Community Guidelines

A little kindness goes a long way.

Nagomi is a place where everyone deserves to feel safe, respected and welcomed. These guidelines help us maintain a friendly and supportive environment for everyone. Please take a moment to familiarise yourself with them.

01. Respect Everyone

Treat everyone with kindness, patience and respect, regardless of their background, identity, opinions or experiences.

Harassment, bullying, discrimination, hate speech, personal attacks, slurs and deliberate attempts to make others uncomfortable are not tolerated.

02. Respect Identities

Everyone deserves to have their identity respected. This includes gender identity, sexuality, names and pronouns.

Do not invalidate, mock, question or deliberately misgender someone. Everyone is welcome to explore and express themselves without judgement.

03. Mental Health & Support

Nagomi is a supportive community where people can share their feelings, experiences and struggles. Please approach sensitive conversations with compassion and understanding.

Do not dismiss, shame, mock or invalidate someone's feelings. Avoid giving unsolicited advice, making assumptions about someone's mental health or attempting to diagnose others.

Remember that we are a peer-support community, not a replacement for professional mental health care or emergency services.

04. Boundaries & Personal Space

Respect everyone's personal boundaries. If someone asks you to stop a conversation, leave them alone or give them space, respect that request.

Do not pressure others into sharing personal information, discussing uncomfortable topics, participating in conversations or interacting with you.

No unwanted flirting, love bombing, emotional manipulation or pressuring others for attention.

05. No E-Dating

Nagomi is a community for friendship, support and shared interests, not a dating server.

E-dating, romantic roleplaying directed at other members, relationship-seeking behaviour and pressuring people into romantic interactions are not permitted.

Friendly interactions are welcome, but please keep things respectful and appropriate.

06. Privacy & Confidentiality

Respect the privacy of everyone in the community. Do not share another person's private messages, personal information, images or conversations without their permission.

Do not share personal information belonging to yourself or others that could compromise safety. This includes addresses, phone numbers, passwords and other sensitive information.

07. Appropriate Content

Keep conversations and shared content appropriate for the community. Explicit sexual content, pornography, graphic violence and excessively disturbing material are not permitted.

Use appropriate channels for their intended purposes, and provide warnings where discussions involve potentially sensitive subjects.

08. Keep Things Friendly

Disagreements happen, and everyone is entitled to their own opinions. However, discussions should remain respectful.

Do not intentionally provoke arguments, start unnecessary drama, spread rumours or attempt to create conflict between members.

If a disagreement becomes uncomfortable, step away or contact a staff member.

09. No Spam or Advertising

Avoid excessive messaging, repeated messages, unsolicited mentions, flooding channels or disruptive behaviour.

Advertising, self-promotion, server invitations and unsolicited promotional links require staff permission.

10. Follow Discord's Guidelines

All members must follow Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines while participating in Nagomi.

This includes following applicable age requirements and avoiding prohibited or harmful behaviour.

11. Listen to Staff

Staff members are here to help maintain a safe and welcoming environment. Please cooperate with reasonable moderation requests.

Do not intentionally evade punishments, bypass restrictions, impersonate staff or interfere with moderation.

If you disagree with a moderation decision, please discuss it respectfully through the appropriate channels.

12. Use Common Sense

Not every situation can be covered by a written rule. Please use your best judgement and consider how your actions may affect others.

If something feels inappropriate, harmful or uncomfortable, it probably deserves a second thought.

When in doubt, reach out to a member of staff.

Final note

You are valued!

We trust our staff to make the right choices when rules are broken. However, even the most exerienced staff can make mistakes. If you feel that a staff member has made an unfair decision, you are incouraged to appeal the punishment and if you feel you are being harrased or targeted in any way, please reach out to the owner!

You can find staff details, including the owner, in the staff page.
