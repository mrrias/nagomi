Mental Health

Nagomi has set a mental health section for venting, general chats, resources and support. Our caring community help to carry each other through hard times and our support staff provide more one to one care for members going through a particularly rough time.

×

Pride

Nagomi has a strong lgbtqia+ community pressense and we intend to keep it that way. We welcome queer chats throughout the entire server but we do also provide more private sections for lgbtqia+ conversation. Our pride section offers resources, lgbt news, general chats and much more for you to check out.

×

Anime & Manga

Nagomi carries underlying anime themes so of course we have a section for all out anime fans. Here we discuss genres, anime, manga, favs and all your hot takes. We also hold anime streams and events for all our hardcore anime fans.

×

Gaming

Gaming is a big part of many Discord users day to day, so we had to include it here. We offer chats, voice channels, events and more for all our gamers in the server. Join and discuss or play favourite games.
