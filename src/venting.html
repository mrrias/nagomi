Venting

Our server offers support for people 13+ as per Discord ToS. We believe everyone should be able to have a safe place to open up and get support.
That's why here at Nagomi our venting is 100% anonymous. Our custom bot connects members with support staff without ever storing any user information. At Nagomi no staff can access information about users using our vents so you can feel at ease without the stress of the exposure that you may feel when openning up to someone normally.

Rules

Our vent rules are based around our server rules. No nsfw, toxic chats, or anything against Discords ToS.
Our support staff are trained in active listenning and support. However, our staff are not professionals and do not handle crisis situations. If you are in a crisis our staff will provide some resources for you before closing the chat.

Getting Started

Our venting is run through our custom bot Yasumi. If you would like to start a vent please use Yasumi's /vent command. You can then tell staff what is on your mind while you wait for someone to take your chat.
